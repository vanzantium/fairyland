# Music

Drop your own audio files here (`.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.flac`,
`.webm`, `.opus`, `.aac`). That's the whole setup.

The ♪ on the canvas plays them through the Proper Shuffle: a golden-ratio
walk through your library — patterned discovery, never random, never
recommended. Three tracks, then enforced silence, then one material
anchor word, then the music ends. Session-bound only.

Nothing here is uploaded, scanned for metadata, or analysed. The library
is whatever this folder contains, and the player never instructs.

You can point somewhere else with the `FAIRYLAND_MUSIC_DIR` environment
variable.
